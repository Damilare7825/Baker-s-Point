## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you give us directly
When you use our **Order & Booking form**, we ask you to provide:
- Your name
- Your phone number
- The item(s) you'd like to order and quantity
- Your preferred date
- Any order notes, cake message, or dietary/design preferences you choose to share

When you contact us via a **WhatsApp link** on the Site (including the "Chat with us" button or the WhatsApp contact rows on our Contact page), any information you share in that WhatsApp conversation is provided directly to us.

### 1.2 Information we do not collect
We do not ask for or store payment card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through the Site. Payment arrangements are discussed and confirmed directly with you over WhatsApp, outside the Site itself.

### 1.3 Automatically collected information
The Site does not currently use cookies, analytics tools, or advertising trackers. We do not automatically collect browsing behavior, device identifiers, or IP-based tracking data.

The Site does load fonts from third-party font providers (Google Fonts and Fontshare) so that pages display correctly. Loading these fonts causes your browser to make a request to those providers' servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as is typical for any request to a web server. We do not control this logging and it is governed by the respective provider's own privacy policy:
- Google Fonts: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Fontshare (by Indian Type Foundry): [https://www.fontshare.com/privacy](https://www.fontshare.com/privacy)

If this changes in the future (for example, if we add analytics or advertising tools), we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide through the Booking/Order form and WhatsApp solely to:
- Confirm availability of the item(s) you've requested
- Prepare, package, and fulfil your order
- Arrange pick up logistics
- Communicate with you about your order (confirmations, changes, delays)
- Respond to general enquiries about our menu, pricing, or custom cake requests
- Improve our products and service based on customer feedback

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

---

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

### 3.1 WhatsApp / Meta
When you submit the Booking form, the Site generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message containing the details you entered (name, phone, order details) and opens WhatsApp so you can review and send it to us yourself. Once sent, that message and the resulting conversation are handled within WhatsApp, which is operated by WhatsApp Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs how that platform processes your message: [https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

We keep records of customer orders and WhatsApp conversations as needed to fulfil orders, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

### 3.2 Other third parties
We do not share your personal information with any other third party, except:
- Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
- With professional advisors (e.g., accountants) where necessary for our business operations, under confidentiality obligations

## 4. Data Retention

We retain order-related information and WhatsApp conversation records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your order, handle any follow-up questions or complaints, and meet our own business, accounting, or legal record-keeping needs. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see Section 6), and we will do so unless we're required to keep it for a legitimate business or legal reason.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is 100% secure, and messaging platforms like WhatsApp operate under their own security model outside our direct control. We encourage you not to share sensitive information (such as full payment card numbers) through the Site or WhatsApp.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection law — including Nigeria's Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) and, where applicable, other regional privacy laws — to:
- Know what personal information we hold about you
- Request a copy of your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Object to certain uses of your information
- Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

## 7. Children's Privacy
